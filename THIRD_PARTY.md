# External material

Example `FFCM2.yaml` inputs are reference mechanism data from the FFCM2 project (https://web.stanford.edu/group/haiwanglab/FFCM2/). Existing headers and attribution are preserved. Check its redistribution terms before publishing these files.

Cantera, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, PyYAML, and SDToolbox are external dependencies with their own licenses; they are not vendored by this download. Reduced mechanism inputs were copied from the supplied research examples; this project does not assign new rights to them.
